CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

What libXp collects

libXp follows file links on the page you submit—not the whole site. It does not sign in to private accounts.

01

PDF and DOCX documents

Linked files in these formats are collected from the submitted page.

02

Public Google Drive files

Publicly downloadable Drive files are supported; sign-in or confirmation pages are not.

03

Downloaded to your device

libXp packages collected files into one ZIP archive for your browser to save.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How long does a harvest take?

It depends on how many files the page links to, their sizes, and how quickly the source site responds. libXp shows an elapsed timer while it works; there is no fixed completion time.

Do I need to keep this page open?

Yes. Keep this tab open until libXp starts the ZIP download. The current service does not provide background job tracking.

Where are the collected files saved?

libXp packages the collected files into one ZIP archive and sends it to your browser. Your browser saves it to its configured downloads location or asks where you want to save it.

Can libXp collect private or sign-in-required files?

No. libXp does not sign in to websites or Google Drive. A file that requires authentication or a Google Drive confirmation step cannot be collected.
